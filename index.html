Features

How it works

Three steps from broke to "broke but posting"

No accounts, no wallets, no blockchain. Just a Windows app and your imagination.

1

Download & launch on Windows

Grab the installer from the download page, run it on Windows 10 or 11, and open Larp Wallet For Fun. It's a lightweight, offline-first app.

2

Pick a preset or build your own

Choose a fake balance, generate transaction history, and select a crypto or credit card layout. Tweak names, currencies, and colors until it looks right.

3

Export the screenshot and post it

Hit export, get a clean PNG mockup, and bring it to the group chat. Remember: it's a larp tool, not a statement of net worth.
